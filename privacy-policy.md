# Privacy Policy — Apna Jugaad (Premium App Store)

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Apna Jugaad ("the Store", package `com.premium.app`) is an app store that
lists APKs for download. This policy explains what data the app collects
and how advertising works.

## 1. Data we collect

- **Reviews, ratings and app requests:** when you leave a review or request
  an app, the text you type and a basic device identifier are stored in
  Firebase (Google) so the store owner can read and manage them.
- **Usage statistics:** download counts, view counts and daily visits are
  counted in Firebase to show trending apps. No personal identity is
  attached to these counts.
- **AI features:** some store features use Google's Gemini API. Text you
  send to those features is processed by Google under Google's own terms.
- **Downloads:** APK files you download are saved on your device by the
  app's download manager. The store does not read other files on your device.

We do **not** collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts,
location, photos, or payment details. We do not sell any data.

## 2. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The Android app shows video ads from Unity Ads:

- **Interstitial video ad** — at several points while you use the
  store: when you open an app's detail page, when you tap Download,
  when a download starts, after a download completes, and before an
  app request is sent. No ad appears on app open.
- **Rewarded video ad** — only when you yourself tap "Boost" in the
  Request App screen; the boost applies only if you watch the ad fully.

There is no banner ad and no ad on app open — Unity Ads has no
App Open format.

Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) may collect advertising identifiers and
usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Unity's Privacy
Policy (https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy). You can reset your
advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings
(Settings → Privacy → Ads).

The website version of the store (GitHub Pages) shows no Unity ads.

## 3. Third-party services

## 4. Children's privacy

The store is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent and
believe your child has sent us personal information, contact us and we
will delete it.
